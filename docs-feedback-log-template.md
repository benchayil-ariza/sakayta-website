# SakayTa — Prototype User Feedback Log

**Instructions:** Recruit at least 3 real people to test the prototype (classmates, family, anyone who isn't your groupmate). Sit with each one, give them the tasks below **without helping them**, and fill this in honestly based on what actually happened.
