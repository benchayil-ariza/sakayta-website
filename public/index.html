About SakayTa

SakayTa is a ride-booking platform made for local communities. It connects passengers with tricycle and habal-habal drivers, with the fare shown before you book and live tracking during the trip.

Drivers submit their license for verification before they can receive ride requests. From booking to drop-off, you always know where your trip stands.

Questions people ask

Quick answers about fares, drivers, and your rides.

How is my fare worked out?

You see an estimated fare before you confirm. It is based on the distance between your pickup and your drop-off.

How are drivers checked?

Each driver submits a license number, and it is checked through a license verification service before they can receive ride requests.

Can I cancel a ride?

Yes. You can cancel a ride while it is still pending or assigned, from your Overview or from My Rides.

How do I become a driver?

Sign up and choose Driver. Then set up your driver profile and submit your license. Once it is verified, go Online to start receiving requests.

Why does SakayTa ask for my location?

Drivers share their location while they are Online, so nearby requests can find them and passengers can follow the trip. Passengers choose their pickup point on the map.
